Forja app icon (every file in this directory).

The icon is a portrait of Gustave Eiffel cut out, converted to black and white and placed over the
app colour. It belongs to the Nodalia icon family: one historical figure per app.

Source portrait: "Gustave Eiffel 1888 Nadar2.jpg" by Nadar (1888), from Wikimedia Commons
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gustave_Eiffel_1888_Nadar2.jpg). Status of the source:
public domain.
